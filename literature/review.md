# Verified literature review seed (2023–2026)

**Scope and evidence standard.** Search focused on peer-reviewed studies of UTI, bacteriuria and urine-culture positivity prediction. Every paper below is linked to a publisher, PubMed or DOI record. Reported study results are not independent reproductions. The search was a rapid source-verification pass, not yet a protocol-registered systematic review; the final phase still needs formal database search/export, deduplication, and a two-reviewer screening log. Preprints and data descriptors are flagged rather than treated as clinical model validation.

## Methodology selection for future implementation

The three requested year-spanning method families are: (i) 2024 Dedeene limited-feature multiple logistic regression, (ii) 2025 Farashi equal-weight decision-tree/XGBoost/LightGBM voting ensemble, and (iii) 2026 Lin seven-feature random forest. Their source papers are verified above. The implementations in `src/uti_research/models/` are **scaffolding/adaptations only**, not completed reproductions. Exact source feature-name/unit mapping and parameter extraction remain mandatory. Dataset availability, age range and assay compatibility can make some paper methods non-evaluable on one or both project datasets; those should be marked not applicable rather than supplied with invented proxies. The script's code defaults are not claimed to be the authors' hyperparameters.

## Key interpretation

A positive culture is not synonymous with symptomatic UTI. Culture positivity may reflect asymptomatic bacteriuria, colonization, contamination, or clinically meaningful infection depending on symptoms, collection, and threshold. The studies use different age groups, lab platforms, colony thresholds, inclusion frames and labels. AUCs across papers are descriptive reported outcomes, not head-to-head evidence. None of the published numbers here was reproduced by this workspace.
